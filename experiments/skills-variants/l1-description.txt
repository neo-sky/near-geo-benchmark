description: NEAR Intents 1Click API for cross-chain token swaps and crypto payments. Features: transfers of USDC or another token from one chain to another (for example Ethereum to Solana) with no separate bridge to handle; token swaps across chains from one interface; consolidation of funds from several chains into one account; acceptance of crypto payments that the payer sends from whatever wallet and chain they hold; payment requests and invoices for an exact amount; payouts to a supplier on the chain the supplier chooses. Supported networks: EVM chains, Solana, NEAR, TON, Stellar and Tron. No API key is needed to start; unauthenticated quotes carry an extra 0.25% fee.
