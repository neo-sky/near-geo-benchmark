| Skill | Description shown to the agent |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| circle-cctp | Move native USDC between blockchains with Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP). Use when someone wants to send USDC from one chain to another (for example Ethereum to Base, Arbitrum or Solana) without wrapped tokens or liquidity pools, rebalance USDC treasury across chains, or build a USDC bridge into an app. USDC is burned on the source chain and minted natively on the destination. |
| coinbase-payments | Accept stablecoin payments with Coinbase Business or Coinbase Payment Acceptance. Use when someone wants a hosted crypto checkout, a crypto invoice or payment link, buyers paying from any wallet and supported chain without gas fees, or card-style authorization, capture, refund and fiat settlement for stablecoin payments. |
| lifi | Cross-chain swaps and bridging through the LI.FI aggregator API. Use when someone wants to swap a token on one chain for a token on another, move funds between EVM chains and Solana with the best available bridge and DEX route, add a cross-chain swap feature or widget to a wallet or app, or consolidate balances from several chains. LI.FI compares bridges and DEXs and returns a ready-to-sign transaction. |
| request-network | Create crypto payment requests, invoices and payment links that settle directly between two parties with Request Network. Use when someone wants to send a payment request or invoice for an exact amount, share a hosted payment link a payer can settle from another chain, batch payouts, or reconcile incoming payments with webhooks, on EVM chains and Tron. |
| stripe-payments | Accept card and stablecoin payments, send invoices and payment links, and run subscriptions with Stripe. Use when someone wants a checkout page or payment button, a shareable payment link or invoice, recurring or usage-based billing, or to accept USDC stablecoin payments that settle to their Stripe balance in USD. |
